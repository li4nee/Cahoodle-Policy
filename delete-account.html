Delete your Cahoodle account

This page explains how to delete your Cahoodle account (Android package com.cahoodle.app) and the data tied to it.

Option 1: delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Cahoodle and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. If asked, sign in again with Google first.

Option 2: request deletion by email

Can't open the app? Email REPLACE_WITH_SUPPORT_EMAIL from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Cahoodle account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, user ID, partner nickname.
  • Your Google sign-in link to Cahoodle (your Firebase Authentication record).
  • If you are paired with a partner: the shared data of the pair, which is game results, custom questions and live game sessions. The pairing is removed, so your partner's profile is unlinked from you.
  • Any open invite code you created.

What is kept

Nothing tied to your account is kept after deletion. Your Google account itself is not affected. Routine server backups or logs kept by our hosting provider (Google Firebase) may hold copies for a short period, up to 90 days, before they are overwritten.

Delete only some data

You can delete individual custom questions in the app at any time. Unlinking from your partner in the app deletes the shared game results, custom questions and sessions without deleting your account.
